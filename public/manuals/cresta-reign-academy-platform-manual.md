# Cresta Reign Academy platform manual

## Access and credentials

Applicants do not create accounts. Start at `/apply`, complete the form, save the reference number, and track progress with the reference number and the email used on the application.

There are no public demo passwords. Staff and parent credentials are issued by an authorised Cresta Reign Academy administrator and verified through the existing Supabase authentication flow. Never share passwords in chat or in this document.

## Staff command centre — `/admin`

1. Open `/admin`.
2. Enter the staff email and password issued by the school.
3. Use the navigation for Content, Admissions, Students, Finance, Messages and Admissions setup.
4. Sign out after using a shared computer.

## Admissions operations — `/admin/admissions`

Search by applicant, reference or guardian. Filter by status and intake. Open an application to review the learner profile and academic history. Use export for a working report. Status changes should reflect the latest decision recorded by the admissions office.

## Student register — `/admin/students`

Search the register by learner or class. Review attendance, average performance and support alerts. Confirm learner information with the school record before taking action.

## Finance workspace — `/admin/finance`

Review the day-school fee structure, balances and recent transactions. Confirm payment details before recording a payment. Use report export for reconciliation, and keep receipts with the school finance records.

## Parent portal — `/parent`

Use the parent account issued by the school. Review the learner summary, attendance, subject progress, fee balance, reports and notices. Contact the school if a learner is missing or information is incorrect.

## Teacher workspace — `/teacher`

Use the staff account issued by the school. Review assigned classes and timetable, mark attendance, enter results, monitor assignments and follow up on outstanding teaching tasks.

## Application flow

1. Enquire or call the office.
2. Complete the public application.
3. Save the generated reference number.
4. Admissions staff review the application.
5. The office contacts the family about the decision and next steps.
6. Bring original documents after an offer when requested.

## Known limitations

- The public manuals document the current interface; they do not grant access.
- Public applicants cannot currently edit a submitted application from the public site.
- Parent and teacher workspaces require the appropriate provisioned account; no passwords are embedded in the application.
- Some content pages depend on published records in the content database and may show an empty state when no record has been published.
- Finance, student and dashboard records require the connected backend and appropriate staff permissions; the UI should not be treated as an offline database.
